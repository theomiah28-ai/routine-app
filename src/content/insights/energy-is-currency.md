Every day you wake up with a fixed amount of energy. Where it goes decides what you build.

## The leak

Cheap dopamine (scrolling, porn, junk food) spends that energy on nothing. You feel tired without having done anything.

## The redirect

Transmutation is the practice of noticing the urge and **pointing it at something**: a workout, a deep-work session, a video you've been putting off.

## Try it today

The next time an urge hits, open the app and tap **Transmute an urge**. Breathe for sixty seconds, then start one of the options it gives you.
