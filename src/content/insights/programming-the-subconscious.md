Most of what you do runs below conscious thought. The subconscious learns from repetition and emotion.

## What feeds it

What you look at first thing in the morning and last thing at night. What you say about yourself. What you rehearse in your mind.

## What to do

Visualise the finished goal. Write it down daily. Repeat it until it stops feeling like a wish and starts feeling like a plan.
