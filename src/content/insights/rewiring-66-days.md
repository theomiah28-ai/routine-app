Your brain changes with what you repeat. Each time you act, the pathway behind that action gets a little stronger.

## Why it feels hard at first

New habits run on weak pathways. Old habits run on motorways. The first weeks feel like effort because they are.

## Why it gets easier

Research on habit formation puts the average at around two months before a behaviour feels automatic. Keep showing up and the effort drops.
